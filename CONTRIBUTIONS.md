## Working agreement
Teams should:
- create issues for substantive work;
- use scoped branches;
- make meaningful commits;
- open PRs for integrated changes;
- review each other's work;
- avoid one final bulk upload.

## Example branches
- `feature/api-client`
- `feature/filing-pagination`
- `feature/incremental-load`
- `feature/company-models`
- `test/data-quality`

## Example commits
- `feat: add authenticated Companies House client`
- `feat: paginate filing history endpoint`
- `fix: prevent duplicate filings on refresh`
- `test: validate company filing references`
- `docs: document incremental load strategy`
